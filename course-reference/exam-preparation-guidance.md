## Authoritative Exam Guidance

- The Learning Objectives shown on the lecture slides are what students will be tested on.
- The objectives follow the Bloom schemata.
- The exam page repeats the learning objectives from the slides.
- Gray lines beneath an objective are example tasks or questions that match the objective and may appear on the exam.
- Students should use the Learning Objectives on the exam page as their primary exam-study guide.

## Example Objective-To-Task Mapping

Learning objective: Define what the security model is.

Possible exam task: A security model consists of which other models?

Expected answer: The trust model and threat model.

## Content-Creation Rules

When creating or revising study guides, exam questions, answer keys, and flash cards:

- Preserve the exact Learning Objective wording from the slides or exam page.
- Make practice questions test the objective at its intended Bloom level rather than only asking for a definition.
- Use the gray-line examples as patterns for question construction, not as the sole question bank.
- Keep answers course-specific and concise. For the security-model example, the answer is the trust model and threat model.
